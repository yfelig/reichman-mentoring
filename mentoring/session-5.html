📚 Book Outline
Generating for Kids
An overview of products for kids in the generative era.
1
How Making Fart Noises Landed Me My First Job
As an ADHD kid, I was smart but couldn't sit through class — by 10th grade I was a dropout. The school system wasn't for me. Self-worth started building when I stopped trying to succeed at school and started doing what I was passionate about. That path led me to the military, psychology, the HCI Lab, and UG Labs.
Personal Story
2
The Parental Dilemma
We can't talk about kids without talking about their parents. What is their motivation? Why do they succeed or fail at guiding their kids? What is the current path of least resistance for handling children — and what does that reveal about what parents actually need?
Research
3
חנוך לנער על פי דרכו vs חוסך שבטו שונא בנו
The educational dilemma — then and today. Two ancient frameworks for raising children, still in tension across every classroom, app, and parenting philosophy. What has changed, and what hasn't?
Culture & History
4
Experiences: What's Actually Being Built for Kids?
An overview of the landscape — entertainment, education, and the gap between what's designed for kids and what kids actually use. Who's winning, and why?
Landscape
5
Value: A Look at the Market
Where is the money? Who is paying, who is deciding, and who benefits? A clear-eyed look at the economics of building for kids.
Market
6
The Moats: Why Building for Kids Is Hard
The real challenges: cognitive variety across ages, short attention spans and non-utilitarian motivations, and the legal landscape — regulation, liability, and risk. Understanding these isn't optional; it's the entry ticket.
Challenges
7
Creation: What Should Be Built?
Given everything we know about the landscape, the parents, the market, and the moats — what is actually worth creating? A framework for deciding what to build and what to skip.
Vision
8
Method: How to Create for Kids
Hint: don't just listen. Kids are terrible at telling you what they want — but brilliant at showing you. Stories from the workshop: what 1,200+ hours with real kids taught about iteration, observation, and the moments that actually matter.
Method
9
Pre and Post AI: What Changes?
A Yuval Noah Harari-style look at the before and after. What did childhood, play, learning, and parenting look like before generative AI entered the picture — and what is fundamentally different now? Not hype, not fear: a clear-eyed account of the shift.
Before & After
10
Promising Developments
What is already working? The early signals, the products and teams that get it right, and the technology shifts that are opening new doors for the first time.
Near Future
11
Moonshot: Where Is This Leading Us?
The long view. If we get this right — if we build experiences that are truly valuable and beneficial for kids — what does the world look like? The optimistic case, and what it would take to get there.
Moonshot
